## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-31

### Added

- Thread-safe in-memory key/value storage.
- Per-key and default TTL expiration.
- LRU eviction based on a configurable item limit.
- Batch operations, conditional writes, and atomic counters.
- Cache statistics and explicit expired-entry cleanup.
